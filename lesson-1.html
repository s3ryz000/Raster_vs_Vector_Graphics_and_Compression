Pixels vs. Paths

Raster images: a grid of pixels

A raster image is a grid of tiny squares called pixels. Each pixel stores one colour. Enlarging a raster stretches its pixels, so it turns blocky.

Vector images: shapes made from maths

A vector image stores instructions such as “draw a circle here”. The computer redraws the shapes at any size, so they always stay sharp.

Sound is digital media too

Audio files store thousands of samples per second, much like an image stores pixels.
